Presented a poster in the 3rd Workshop on Parametric Phenomena held in Konstanz, Germany. The event gathered researchers from around the world to discuss how parametric effects show up in all sorts of experimental systems. It was a great chance to exchange ideas, get feedback, and connect with people working on similar problems. Details can be accessed in the website <a href="https://www.pep.ethz.ch" target="_blank">2025 Parametric Phenomena Workshop official website</a>
